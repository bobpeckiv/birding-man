Saturday, April 10, 2027

One day, two moods

Plate No. 1

Field & Forum

Afternoon — family friendly
  • A live birds of prey demonstration
  • Short talks from Travis Audubon and local experts
  • A look at Austin's purple martin housing volunteers
  • Bird-inspired poetry and creative readings
Plate No. 2

Roost & Revel

Evening — art, music, and a party
  • A juried exhibition of bird-inspired art
  • Live performance, a DJ set, and the bird costume contest
  • Original work for sale, direct from the artists
  • Merch and prints from the Birding Man shop
  • Good company, low key, all birders welcome

March 28, 2026

Year one, in review

Birding Man opened at ArtUs Co with an afternoon of birds of prey, field talks, and poetry, then closed the gallery for an evening art party with live performance, a DJ set, and a bird costume contest with prizes for the top three.

67 artists

A juried group exhibition honoring the avian world, up on the walls from March 28 through May 30.

4 speakers

Talks by Bryan Olivas-Orozco, Cameron Kennedy, Jen Bristol, and Travis Audubon, plus a live birds of prey demo.

1 costume contest

Performance by Jenna Jasso, music by DJ Chappie Funk, and bird-inspired costumes judged for top honors.
